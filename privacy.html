Privacy Policy

AAT Prep · Last updated: 19 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AAT Prep (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no third-party analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

2. Data stored on your device

Study progress (answers, streak, bookmarks, mock history, unit mastery, preferences, exam date) is stored only on your device using on-device app storage. It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the App.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit and the App Store) on iPhone and iPad, or by Google Play Billing on Android. We never see or receive payment card details. Purchase data is governed by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy.

4. Notifications

If you enable the optional daily study reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. This is handled on-device by iOS or Android and is not sent to us or any third party. Disable anytime in the App or in your device settings.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites and do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). The App does not require Camera, Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone, or Health access.

6. Children

The App is an adult education study aid for accounting technician exam candidates. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Apple Account or Google account holders manage purchases.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
